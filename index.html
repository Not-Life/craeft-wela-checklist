Business & legal

  • Business account
    • Keeps store money separate, which makes taxes much easier.
    • Open it under the LLC name with the EIN. Bring the LLC paperwork to the bank.
    • Many banks and credit unions have free small-business checking.
    Personal account
    • Fastest – nothing to open.
    • Store money and personal money mix together, so tracking sales for taxes takes more work.
    • Can switch to a business account later.

Shopify setup

  • BasicBest fit
    • $39 a month, or $29 a month if paid yearly ($348 up front).
    • Keeps the full website as it is now.
    • Card fee: 2.9% + 30¢ per online sale.
    • Fine for one owner and a few products.
    Grow
    • $105 a month, or $79 a month if paid yearly.
    • Slightly lower card fees (2.7% + 30¢) and up to 5 staff logins.
    • Only worth it with a lot of sales – more than this shop needs to start.
    Starter
    • $5 a month.
    • Does not include the website. It only gives product links to share on social media, so the pages Life built would go away.
    • Not recommended for this shop.

  • Don't type a password here. Give it to Life in person.
